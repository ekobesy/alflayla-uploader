AlfLayla Uploader

AlfLayla Uploader is a private tool used by the owner of the النديم (Alnadim Tales) YouTube channel to upload his own animated episodes of One Thousand and One Nights to his own channel.

It runs only on the owner's own computers and uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set titles and descriptions, add thumbnails and subtitles, and add videos to the channel's playlist. It is not offered to, and cannot be used by, anyone else.
