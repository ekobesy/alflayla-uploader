Privacy Policy - AlfLayla Uploader

Last updated: November 14, 2026

AlfLayla Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the النديم (Alnadim Tales) YouTube channel to manage his own channel. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app is allowed to manage his own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API: upload videos, set titles, descriptions and other video settings, upload thumbnails and subtitles, and add videos to the channel's playlists. It does not access any other Google data and does not access anyone else's account.

How data is stored

The sign-in token issued by Google is stored only on the channel owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database. No personal data is collected from viewers or from any other person.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it shows no ads. The only data sent anywhere is the channel owner's own content, sent to YouTube for upload.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the token file on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the النديم YouTube channel.
