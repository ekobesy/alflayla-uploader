Privacy Policy - AlfLayla Uploader

Last updated: November 17, 2026

AlfLayla Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the النديم (Alnadim Tales) YouTube channel, Facebook Page and Instagram account to publish his own videos there. It is not offered to the public.

What the app accesses

YouTube. When the channel owner signs in with Google, the app is allowed to manage his own YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API: upload videos, set titles, descriptions and other video settings, upload thumbnails and subtitles, and add videos to the channel's playlists. It does not access any other Google data and does not access anyone else's account.

Facebook and Instagram. When the owner signs in with Facebook, the app is allowed to publish videos, reels, captions and comments to his own Facebook Page النديم and to the Instagram professional account linked to that Page, through Meta's Graph API. It reads only what it needs to do that: the list of Pages he manages, the linked Instagram account, and the status of the posts it creates. It does not read messages, does not access other people's profiles, and does not collect data about followers or viewers.

How data is stored

The sign-in tokens issued by Google and Meta are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database. No personal data is collected from viewers, followers or any other person.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties, and it shows no ads. The only data sent anywhere is the owner's own content, sent to YouTube, Facebook and Instagram for publishing.

YouTube API Services

This app uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Google: access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Facebook and Instagram: access can be removed at any time from Facebook Settings → Business Integrations (https://www.facebook.com/settings/?tab=business_tools) by removing "AlfLayla Uploader".

Deleting the token files on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.

How to delete your data

The app stores no data about anyone other than the owner, and keeps nothing on any server. To delete everything the app holds:

  1. Remove "AlfLayla Uploader" in Facebook Settings → Business Integrations, and in your Google Account → Third-party apps & services.
  2. Delete the token files on the owner's computer (the app's cache\meta and cache\yt folders).

Posts and videos published with the app are ordinary posts on YouTube, Facebook and Instagram and can be deleted there like any other post. If you believe the app holds data about you, send a request through the contact link below and it will be answered and deleted within 30 days.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the النديم YouTube channel.
